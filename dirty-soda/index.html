How to Make a Dirty Soda

First popularized in Utah, a dirty soda takes any kind of soda as a base and "dirties" it up by adding things like flavored syrups, fruit puree, and cream. Give it a mix, and you have a fun non-alcoholic drink!

How to Make a Dirty Soda

Grab Your Ingredients

Base Soda
Any soda you like: Dr. Pepper, Sprite, Coke, Cream Soda, etc.
Flavored Syrup
Any flavor you like: vanilla, caramel, strawberry, etc.
Cream
Any plain or flavored cream: half & half, sweet cream, coconut cream, coffee creamer, etc.
Optional Add-Ins
If you want, you can add things like fruit puree or fruit juice (instead of or in addition to the syrup and cream). You might also want to top it with whip or cold foam!

Mix It Up

  1. Fill a glass about halfway with crushed ice.
  2. Add 1-3 pumps of flavor.
  3. (optional) Add your fruit puree.
  4. Fill most of the way with your base soda.
  5. Add a splash of cream or fruit juice.
  6. (optional) Top with whip or cold foam.
  7. Enjoy!
